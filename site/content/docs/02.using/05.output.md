## Live output

stdout and stderr stream into the terminal in real time and are color-coded. The pane follows new output until you scroll up; `End` jumps back to following.

## Scrollback

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `PgUp` `PgDn` | Scroll |
| `Home` | Top |
| `End` | Follow live output again |

The in-memory buffer keeps the most recent lines. Everything, including what scrolled out of the buffer, is also in the [run log](/docs/using/logs).

## Find in output

1. Press `f`, type, `Enter`.
2. Matches are highlighted; `n` goes to an **older** match and `N` to a **newer** one.

## Messy output, handled

- **Non-UTF-8 bytes** don't stop the stream.
- **Carriage-return progress bars** (the ones that redraw one line) are collapsed instead of smearing the pane.
- **ANSI escape codes** are stripped, so colorized tools stay readable.
- **Long lines** are shown in full; the pane shows the exact tail.
